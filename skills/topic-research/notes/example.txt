# Topic: Houseplants
keywords: water, light, soil, leaves, pot, drainage
- Most houseplants prefer bright, indirect light.
- Overwatering is more common than underwatering.
- Water when the top few centimetres of soil feel dry.
- Pots with drainage holes help prevent soggy roots.
- Yellow leaves often point to too much water.
- Brown, crispy leaf tips can mean low humidity.
- Rotate pots every couple of weeks for even growth.
- Dust on leaves reduces how much light they absorb.
- Wipe leaves gently with a damp cloth.
- Most plants grow more slowly in the cooler months.
- Repot when roots circle the bottom of the pot.
- Choose a pot only slightly larger than the old one.
- Succulents need gritty, fast-draining soil.
- Ferns enjoy steady moisture and indirect light.
- Snake plants tolerate low light and infrequent watering.
- Group plants together to raise local humidity.
fact: Many plants can be grown from a single leaf or stem cutting.
source: Indoor Gardening Handbook, chapter 3
